Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

1. Data Collection & Compliance (DPDP Act, India)

In strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India, Elevate Web Studio collects only strictly necessary personal data required to provide our services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data.

2. What Data We Collect & Why

  • Name & Email: Collected solely via our contact form to communicate regarding your project inquiry.
  • Usage Data: We do not use third-party analytics trackers. Basic server logs (IP, browser type) are kept temporarily for security purposes only.

3. User Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data stored by us. Contact us at privacy@elevateweb.in to exercise these rights.

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies
